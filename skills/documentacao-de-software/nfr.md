# Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

NFRs must be **measurable and verifiable**. Never use vague terms without associated metrics.

---

## Writing Standard

| ❌ Bad                                | ✅ Good                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "the system must be fast"            | "endpoint X must respond within 500ms for 95% of requests under 1,000 concurrent users"                   |
| "the system must be secure"          | "all communication must use TLS 1.2+; JWT tokens must expire in 15 minutes"                               |
| "the system must be stable"          | "minimum availability of 99.5% measurable via uptime monitor"                                              |
| "the system must be easy to use"     | "the registration flow must be completable in up to 3 steps, with an abandonment rate below 10%"           |

---

## Recommended Categories

### Performance

- Maximum latency per endpoint/operation (e.g., p95, p99).
- Expected throughput (requests per second, transactions per minute).
- Capacity (data volume, number of records, simultaneous users).
- Scalability: expected behavior under increasing load.

**Example:**
> The `POST /api/v1/orders` endpoint must respond within 300ms (p95) for 500 simultaneous requests.

---

### Security

- Minimum TLS protocol and version.
- Authentication policy (type, token expiration, MFA).
- Authorization control (RBAC, ABAC, scopes).
- Sensitive data protection (masking, encryption at rest and in transit).
- Regulatory compliance (LGPD, PCI-DSS, SOC 2).

**Example:**
> Credit card data must not be stored; only the PAN token generated by the gateway.

---

### Reliability

- Minimum availability (e.g., 99.5% monthly).
- Fault tolerance (graceful degradation, circuit breaker).
- Disaster recovery (defined RTO and RPO).
- Retry policy and idempotency for critical operations.

**Example:**
> The service must maintain 99.9% availability (excluding planned maintenance windows).

---

### Usability and Accessibility

- WCAG compliance (level AA or AAA).
- Maximum time for completing critical tasks.
- Keyboard and screen reader navigation support.
- Minimum contrast (e.g., 4.5:1 for normal text).

**Example:**
> All screens must achieve WCAG 2.1 level AA compliance.

---

### Observability

- Minimum logging level (requests, errors, critical events).
- Distributed tracing (trace IDs, correlation IDs).
- Exposed metrics (latency, error rate, saturation).
- Defined alerts (thresholds and notification channels).

**Example:**
> Every request must generate a structured log with correlation-id, timestamp, method, path, status, and duration.

---

## NFR Section Template in Requirement Documents

```markdown
## Non-Functional Requirements

### Performance
- The `[method] [path]` endpoint must respond within [X]ms for [Y]% of requests under [Z] simultaneous users.

### Security
- [Describe measurable authentication, authorization, encryption, or compliance requirements.]

### Reliability
- Minimum availability: [X]% (monthly/yearly).
- Retry policy: [X] attempts with exponential backoff of [Y]ms.

### Usability
- [Describe usability and accessibility metrics.]

### Observability
- [Describe logging, tracing, and alerting requirements.]
```
